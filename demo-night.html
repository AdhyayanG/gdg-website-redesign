The Format

How Demo Night Runs

A tight, high-energy showcase built to give every founder a fair, memorable shot.

Step 01

Apply

Submit your project through the open application. Pre-seed to Series A, all sectors of Web3 welcome.

Step 02

Get Curated

A panel of operators and investors reviews submissions and selects the finalist lineup for the stage.

Step 03

Pitch Live

Take the stage for a focused live pitch and demo, followed by rapid-fire questions from the judges.

Step 04

Connect

Move straight into investor speed-networking and audience voting. The conversations continue long after.

Run of Show

A Night, Start to Finish

Indicative schedule. Exact timings are confirmed closer to the event.

18:00

Doors & Founder Check-in

Finalists arrive, tech-check their demos, and settle in. Networking opens for the audience.

18:45

Opening & Judge Introductions

The host sets the stage and introduces the panel of investors and operators judging the night.

19:00

Live Pitch Rounds

Finalists take the stage back to back, each with a live pitch, a demo, and a Q&A with the judges.

20:30

Audience Vote & Deliberation

The crowd casts its vote for the audience choice award while judges deliberate on the main prize.

21:00

Winners & After-Hours

Awards are announced, and the room rolls into open networking with investors and fellow founders.

Who Should Apply

Built for Founders With Something to Show

If you have a working product, a bold thesis, and the ambition to build in public, Demo Night is your stage. No gatekeeping, just a fair shot in front of the right people.

  • ✓ Pre-seed to Series A Web3 projects
  • ✓ A live product or working prototype to demo
  • ✓ Founders ready to field investor questions on stage
  • ✓ Teams looking for capital, partners, or visibility
