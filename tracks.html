Seven Tracks. One Carnival.

Every corner of the ecosystem gets its stage. Deep-dive sessions, keynotes, and workshops curated for founders, developers, investors, and creators.

Track 01
Blockchain & Infrastructure
The rails that power the decentralised web, from base-layer consensus to modular scaling.
  • Layer 1 consensus and Layer 2 scalability
  • Modular chains, rollups, and data availability
  • Interoperability and cross-chain bridges
  • Node infrastructure and developer tooling
Core Protocol
Track 02
DAO & Governance
How decentralised communities coordinate capital, decisions, and ownership at scale.
  • On-chain voting and delegation models
  • Treasury management and public goods funding
  • Reputation, identity, and contributor incentives
  • Legal wrappers and regulatory frameworks
Governance
Track 03
Metaverse & GameFi
Where gaming, virtual worlds, and digital asset economies converge on-chain.
  • Play-to-earn and sustainable game economies
  • Interoperable in-game assets and identity
  • Immersive worlds and creator monetisation
  • On-chain gaming infrastructure
Gaming
Track 04
ZK & Security
Privacy-preserving cryptography and the practices that keep protocols trustless.
  • Zero-knowledge proofs and applications
  • Smart contract audits and formal verification
  • MEV, exploits, and mitigation strategies
  • Privacy-preserving compliance
Privacy Tech
Track 05
CeFi / DeFi & Staking
The future of financial infrastructure, from liquid staking to real-world yield.
  • Lending, DEXs, and liquidity provision
  • Liquid staking and restaking economies
  • Stablecoins and on-chain forex
  • Bridging CeFi rails with DeFi liquidity
Finance
Track 06
Enterprise Blockchain
Institutional adoption and the tokenisation of real-world value.
  • Tokenisation of real-world assets
  • Supply chain and provenance tracking
  • Permissioned networks and B2B integration
  • Compliance, custody, and settlement
Enterprise

NFT & Utilities
Beyond digital art, NFTs as access, ownership, and loyalty primitives.
  • NFTs as access tokens and memberships
  • IP ownership and creator royalties
  • Loyalty programs and brand activations
  • Dynamic and utility-first collections
