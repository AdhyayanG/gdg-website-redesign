How Judging Works

Merit First, Hype Second

Every nominee is assessed against a clear, consistent rubric by a panel of operators, investors, and researchers, so the awards mean something the morning after.

  • ✓ Product depth and technical execution
  • ✓ Traction, adoption, and real usage
  • ✓ Originality and contribution to the ecosystem
  • ✓ Clarity of vision and team credibility
